# Getis-Ord Gi\* Hot Spot Analysis

This folder contains the site-level results from the Getis-Ord Gi\* hot spot analysis used to evaluate the spatial distribution of risk. The analysis was carried out in ArcGIS Pro using the final Weighted Linear Combination (WLC) risk score for the analysis field.

### Method

The Getis-Ord Gi\* statistic was used to identify statistically significant clusters of high and low risk values. The analysis compares the local pattern of WLC risk scores for each site with the values of neighboring sites. Positive z-scores indicate clustering of high values and negative z-scores indicate clustering of low values.

The spatial relationship between sites was defined using:

* 8 nearest neighbors
* Euclidean distance

This neighborhood definition was chosen because the study area contains an uneven distribution of sites and a suitable distance-band peak was not found.

### Output

`results/Getis\_Ord\_Gi\_site\_results.csv`

The output contains the site-level Getis-Ord Gi\* results used in the thesis analysis.

The file includes the site identifier and name, the final WLC archaeological risk score, and the Gi\* output fields generated by ArcGIS Pro.

Typical fields include:

|Field|Description|
|-|-|
|`Site\_ID`|Unique archaeological site identifier|
|`Site`|Standardized archaeological site name|
|`WLC\_RISK`|Final archaeological risk score used as the analysis field|
|`GiZScore`|Getis-Ord Gi\* z-score|
|`GiPValue`|Getis-Ord Gi\* p-value|
|`Gi\_Bin`|ArcGIS significance category for statistically significant hot spots and cold spots|

The exact ArcGIS field names are kept in the output.

### Interpretation

The Gi\* classification differentiates statistically significant clusters of high and low risk.

Positive Gi\* categories represent hot spots and negative categories represent cold spots. Sites with values that do not reach the significance thresholds are classified as not statistically significant.

The site-level output was used to summarize the number and percentage of sites in each Gi\* significance category and to map the spatial distribution of significant archaeological risk clusters.

### Relationship to the thesis

The results in this folder support the Getis-Ord Gi\* analysis in Chapter 5 of the thesis, including the summary table of hot spot and cold spot categories and the map showing the spatial distribution of statistically significant risk. The analysis itself was carried out in ArcGIS Pro.

### Data availability

Precise site coordinates are not included in the public output.

The CSV contains the analytical attributes required to inspect the site-level Gi\* results, although it excludes the original spatial coordinates of the archaeological sites.
